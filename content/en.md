## Courses

**Our Online Courses** — Academic Programs

> NEUM offers programs designed for every stage of your musical
> development, from preparation for entry into a Bachelor degree to

CTA: More information · Explore the courses on Campus

### Undergraduate — Preparation for Bachelor entry

For those who wish to access a European or American Bachelor in music. An
academic course preparing for entrance exams in harmony, analysis and ear
training.

- Advanced harmony
- Musical analysis
- Ear training
- Audition preparation

### Bachelor & Master — ABRSM/Trinity College Degrees

For those who wish to obtain with us the official degrees of the
Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music (ABRSM) and Trinity
College. This training lets you complete your higher education
independently.

- Piano
- Theoretical training
- International ABRSM/Trinity certification
- Personalized mentoring

### Seminars — Master Classes

Seminars, master classes and other open activities for advanced students
and professional musicians who wish to continue their training.

- Masterclasses
- Intensive workshops
- Mentorships
- Continuing education
